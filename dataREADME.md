# Raw inputs

The complete download contains the nine supplied files under these canonical names:
wsdr.zip, wcer.zip, wlnd.zip, upcsdr.csv, upccer.csv, upclnd.csv, demo.dta,
ccount.dta and codebook.pdf. ZIP movement files are streamed directly, never
sampled or replaced with precleaned panels. No extraction is necessary.

Data are supplied by the James M. Kilts Center for Marketing at the University
of Chicago Booth School of Business for academic research use. A public GitHub
repository should contain code and permitted derived results; obtain the raw
data from https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/kilts/research-data/dominicks
and check the provider's terms before distributing them. The provided .gitignore
keeps the raw files out of Git.
